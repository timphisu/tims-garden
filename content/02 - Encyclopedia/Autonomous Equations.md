## Definition

> [!definition]
> An ordinary differential equation where the independent variable is not explicitly stated is said to be **autonomous**. An autonomous first-order differential equation can be written as $f(y, y') = 0$ or in normal form as
> 
> $$
> \frac{dy}{dx} = f(y)
> $$

> Another way of looking at this is that the slope of $y(x)$ is dependent on the level that $y$ is at on the graph
## Examples of Autonomous Equations

A famous example is the [[logistic model]] for population growth which expresses the population growth rate as

$$
\frac{dP}{dt} = kP\left( 1-\frac{P}{L} \right)
$$

where

* $k$ is the intrinsic growth rate, that is, the maximum growth rate per capita

* $P$ is the current population number

* $L$ is the carrying capacity
