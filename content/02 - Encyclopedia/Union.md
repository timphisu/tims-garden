> [!definition]
> Let $A$ and $B$ be sets. The *union* of the sets $A$ and $B$, denoted $A \cup B$, is the set that contains those elements that are either in $A$ or in $B$, or in both. 
> 

An element $x$ belongs to the union of the sets $A$ and $B$ if and only if $x$ belongs to $A$ or $x$ belongs to $B$. That is, 

$$
A \cup B = \{ x \ | \ x \in A \lor x \in B \} 
$$

#### Examples of Union

The union of the sets $\{ 1, 3, 5 \}$ and $\{ 1, 2, 3 \}$ is the set $\{ 1, 2, 3, 5 \}$

$$
\{ 1, 3, 5 \} \cup \{ 1, 2, 3 \} = \{ 1, 2, 3, 5 \}
$$

The union of the set of all the relatives on your mother's side and the set of all the relative's on your father's side is the set of your extended family.
