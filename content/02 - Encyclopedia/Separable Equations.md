# Separable Equations

## Form

Separable equations are said to be first-order [[differential equations]] with *separable variables*, which makes it easier to isolate $x$'s and $y$'s to their respective sides and integrate. 

> [!definition]
> A first-order differential equation of the form
> 
> $$
> \frac{dy}{dx} = g(x)h(y)
> $$
> 
> is said to be **separable** or to have **separable variables**.
