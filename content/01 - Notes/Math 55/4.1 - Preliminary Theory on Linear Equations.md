A **general solution** is a family of solutions defined on some interval $I$ that contains *all* solutions of the DE that are defined on $I$.

> [!excerpt]
> Only in the case of *linear* first-order differential equations were we able to obtain general solutions by paying close attention to certain continuity conditions imposed on the coefficients in the equation. Since our goal is to find general solutions of linear higher-order DEs, we first need to examine some of the basic theory of linear equations.
